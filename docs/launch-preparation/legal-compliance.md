# Legal Compliance & Documentation - Taska Platform

## 📋 Terms of Service

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to Taska, a digital platform connecting skilled artisans with clients across South Africa. By using our platform, you agree to these Terms of Service.

### 2. Platform Overview
- **Service:** Online marketplace for artisan services
- **Jurisdiction:** Republic of South Africa
- **Governing Law:** South African law
- **Regulatory Compliance:** POPIA, Consumer Protection Act

### 3. User Categories & Responsibilities

#### 3.1 Clients
- Must be 18+ years or have parental consent
- Responsible for accurate job descriptions
- Must pay for accepted services
- Cannot discriminate based on protected characteristics

#### 3.2 Artisans
- Must possess valid skills and qualifications
- Responsible for quality of work delivered
- Must maintain professional conduct
- Required to complete verification process

#### 3.3 Platform Obligations
- Provide secure payment processing
- Maintain platform availability (99.5% uptime target)
- Protect user data per POPIA requirements
- Facilitate dispute resolution

### 4. Financial Terms
- **Platform Fee:** 15% of transaction value (inclusive of VAT)
- **Payment Terms:** Net 7 days after job completion
- **Refund Policy:** Case-by-case basis within 30 days
- **Currency:** South African Rand (ZAR)

### 5. Prohibited Activities
- Fraudulent or deceptive practices
- Harassment or discriminatory behavior
- Circumventing platform payment systems
- Sharing personal contact information before job acceptance

### 6. Dispute Resolution
- Internal mediation process
- Escalation to independent arbitration
- Right to approach courts for urgent relief
- Maximum resolution timeframe: 30 business days

## ⚖️ Dispute Resolution Framework

### 1. Internal Resolution Process

#### Step 1: Direct Communication (1-3 days)
- Platform provides communication tools
- Users attempt to resolve directly
- Platform provides templates and guidance

#### Step 2: Platform Mediation (5-10 days)
- Taska customer service intervention
- Evidence collection and review
- Proposed resolution with reasoning

#### Step 3: Formal Dispute (10-30 days)
- Written complaint submission
- Independent review panel
- Binding resolution decision

### 2. Escalation Options
- CGSO (Consumer Goods and Services Ombud)
- Small Claims Court (under R20,000)
- High Court for larger disputes
- Alternative dispute resolution providers

### 3. Evidence Requirements
- Communication records
- Photos of work performed
- Payment confirmations
- Platform activity logs
