## 👤 Client User Guide

### Getting Started

#### 1. Creating Your Account
1. Visit [www.taska.co.za](https://www.taska.co.za)
2. Click "Sign Up" and select "I need services"
3. Enter your email and create a strong password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile with:
   - Full name and contact details
   - South African address
   - Phone number (for SMS verification)

#### 2. Posting Your First Job
1. Click "Post a Job" from your dashboard
2. **Step 1:** Choose your service category
   - Browse 12+ categories (Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry, etc.)
   - Select the most specific category for better matches
3. **Step 2:** Describe your job
   - Write a clear, detailed description
   - Upload photos showing the work needed
   - Specify timeline and urgency
4. **Step 3:** Set your budget
   - Use our budget calculator for guidance
   - Consider VAT and platform fees
   - Set realistic expectations
5. **Step 4:** Add location details
   - Enter your complete address
   - Specify access requirements
   - Note parking availability
6. **Step 5:** Review and publish
   - Check all details carefully
   - Save as draft if not ready
   - Publish to receive bids

#### 3. Managing Bids
1. **Receiving Bids:**
   - Get notified via email and SMS
   - View bids in your dashboard
   - See artisan profiles and ratings

2. **Comparing Bids:**
   - Use the comparison table
   - Check artisan verification status
   - Review portfolios and past work
   - Consider price, timeline, and ratings

3. **Accepting a Bid:**
   - Select the best bid for your needs
   - Payment will be held in escrow
   - Other bidders are automatically notified
   - Work agreement is generated

#### 4. Payment & Completion
1. **Payment Process:**
   - Payment secured in escrow upon bid acceptance
   - Funds released after job completion
   - Download invoices from your dashboard
   - VAT receipts provided automatically

2. **Job Completion:**
   - Mark job as complete when satisfied
   - Leave a detailed review and rating
   - Photos of completed work appreciated
   - Payment automatically released to artisan

#### 1. Registration & Verification
1. **Create Account:**

   - Complete detailed profile
   - Upload profile photo
   - Add contact information

2. **Specialization Setup:**
   - Choose your service categories

   - Upload South African ID

1. **Job Feed:**
   - Browse available jobs in your area

2. **Search Filters:**

   - Create templates for common jobs

3. **Pricing Guidelines:**

2. **Progress Updates:**
   - Send regular progress photos

3. **Job Completion:**

## ❓ FAQ Section

### General Questions

**Q: What is Taska?**
A: Taska is a South African platform connecting skilled artisans with clients who need home services, repairs, and improvements.

**Q: How does Taska work?**
A: Clients post jobs, artisans submit bids, clients choose their preferred artisan, payment is held securely, work is completed, and payment is released.

**Q: Is Taska available throughout South Africa?**
A: Yes, Taska operates in all major cities and towns across South Africa, with particular strength in Johannesburg, Cape Town, and Durban.

### For Clients

**Q: How much does it cost to use Taska?**
A: Posting jobs and receiving bids is free. You only pay for the services you book plus a 15% platform fee (inclusive of VAT).

**Q: How do I know if an artisan is qualified?**
A: All artisans go through verification including ID checks, qualification verification, and portfolio review. Look for the "Verified" badge.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the work?**
A: Contact our support team immediately. We have a dispute resolution process and may provide refunds for unsatisfactory work.

**Q: How is my payment protected?**
A: Payments are held in secure escrow until you confirm the work is completed satisfactorily.

### For Artisans

**Q: How much does Taska cost for artisans?**
A: Registration and bidding are free. Taska charges a 15% fee (inclusive of VAT) only on successful jobs.

**Q: How long does verification take?**
A: Verification typically takes 1-3 business days once all required documents are submitted.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payment is released automatically when the client marks the job as complete, typically within 24 hours.

**Q: Can I work in multiple categories?**
A: Yes, you can offer services in multiple categories if you have the relevant skills and qualifications.

### Technical Support

**Q: I'm having trouble uploading photos. What should I do?**
A: Ensure photos are under 5MB and in JPG, PNG, or HEIC format. Try refreshing the page or using a different browser.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: Can I use Taska on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes, Taska is fully optimized for mobile devices and works on all modern smartphones and tablets.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Login Problems
**Issue:** Can't log in to account
**Solutions:**
1. Check email and password spelling
2. Try "Forgot Password" if needed
3. Clear browser cache and cookies
4. Disable browser extensions temporarily
5. Contact support if problem persists

#### Payment Issues
**Issue:** Payment not processing
**Solutions:**
1. Verify card details and expiry date
2. Check sufficient funds available
3. Try alternative payment method
4. Contact your bank about international transactions
5. Reach out to support for assistance

#### File Upload Problems
**Issue:** Can't upload photos or documents
**Solutions:**
1. Check file size (max 5MB per file)
2. Ensure supported format (JPG, PNG, PDF)
3. Try different browser or device
4. Check internet connection stability
5. Compress large images before uploading

#### Mobile App Issues
**Issue:** App not working properly
**Solutions:**
1. Update to latest app version
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache and data
4. Check available storage space
5. Reinstall app if necessary
